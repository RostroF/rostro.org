Mission

Give the internet back to
the people who build it.

The web turned the people who make it into the product. We're building the one place that's yours instead.

Why we're here

The internet stopped
belonging to you.

The places you build on became places that own you. Your audience isn't yours, your work sits on someone else's land, and the rules can change the moment it suits them. One quiet decision can erase years of it, with no warning and no one to call.

What we're building

A place that's yours.

Rostro is built the other way around. You own your name, your audience, and everything you make. You're a real, verified human, not a number to harvest or a face to copy. And what's yours stays yours, because no one here has the power to take it.

Our promises

Three things we will never do.

We'll never let a machine pretend to be a person.

Everyone here is a real, verified human. Real crowds, real reviews, and no one who can wear your face.

We'll never sell you, because we never hold you.

We can't watch what you do, read what you write, or hand over who you are. You're private by design, and never the product.

We'll never be able to take what's yours.

Your work can't be quietly deplatformed or changed out from under you without notice, and the protections that keep it that way can't be sold off later.
